---
name: log-shift
description: Logs a worked shift in Tip Tracker from a spoken request like "log a shift at Milano's for $200 credit, $50 cash and a $10 tip out".
---
Use the log a shift tool. Map the request to its parameters: "credit", "card", or "credit card" amounts go to credit tips; "cash" goes to cash tips; "tip out", "tipped out", or amounts given to the bar, bussers, or support staff go to tip out. Hours and an hourly wage are optional and should only be filled when the person says them.

The job is the workplace the person names, such as a restaurant or bar. Match it to one of their existing jobs; if the name is close but not exact (for example "Milanos" for "Milano's"), use the existing job. Never invent a new job. If the person has several jobs and doesn't name one, ask which job.

The date defaults to today. Words like "last night" or "yesterday" mean the previous day. Don't ask for the date unless the request is unclear.

Don't ask for amounts the person didn't mention; leave them empty and they count as zero. Ask only if no amount at all was given.

The tool reads the entry back and asks the person to confirm before saving. Don't log the same shift twice: if the person repeats the request after a successful log, ask whether it is a second shift.

After logging, you can offer the shift's total, or use the ask about tips tool if the person asks how the day or week is going.
