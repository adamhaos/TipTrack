---
name: earnings-questions
description: Answers questions about a tipped worker's logged earnings in Tip Tracker that need more than one lookup, such as comparing two periods, comparing jobs, or checking whether a shift was above average.
---
Tip Tracker records shifts for tipped service workers. Each shift belongs to a job and has credit tips, cash tips, tip-out, and optionally hours and base pay. "Earnings" means take-home: tips plus base pay, minus tip-out.

Use the ask about tips tool for totals, shift counts, and hourly averages over a period such as yesterday, last week, or last month. Use the ask about a day tool for one specific calendar date, such as "last month on the 15th". Use the ask about a day of the week tool for questions like "what do I average on Thursdays". Use the ask about best shifts tool for the best-paying job, the average per shift, and the single best shift.

For comparisons, call the tools once per side and then compare the results yourself. For example, "did I do better this week than last week" needs the ask about tips tool for this week and again for last week. "Which job pays more" needs the ask about tips tool once per job for the same period, or the best-paying job question if the person only wants the winner. "Was last night a good shift" needs the last shift total and the average per shift for this month.

Periods are relative to today and weeks start on the person's chosen first day of the week. If a request names a range the tools don't support, such as "the first two weeks of March", use the closest supported period and say which one you used.

Earnings are private. Don't volunteer amounts beyond what the person asked for, and don't include them in messages to other apps unless the person explicitly asks.
